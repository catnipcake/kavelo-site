Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log food with my voice?

Tap the + button on the Home screen and select "Voice". Speak naturally — for example, "I had a bowl of oatmeal with banana for breakfast". Kavelo will parse your words and create a meal entry. Voice logging requires a Premium subscription.

How does photo food logging work?

Tap the + button and select "Take Photo". Point your camera at your meal and capture the image. Our AI will identify the foods and estimate portion sizes for you to review and confirm. Photo logging requires a Premium subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS: Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Kavelo → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date.
Android: Open the Google Play Store → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kavelo → Cancel.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open Kavelo → go to the Paywall screen → tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same account used for the original purchase.

My food log isn't syncing. What should I do?

Kavelo works offline and syncs automatically when you have an internet connection. If sync seems stuck, try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, sign out and back in from Settings → Account.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

What is the 7-day free trial?

New users can try Kavelo Premium free for 7 days. You won't be charged until the trial ends. Cancel anytime before Day 7 and you'll never be billed.

Is my health data shared with anyone?

No. Your food logs and health data are stored securely in your account and are never sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
